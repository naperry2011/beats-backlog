# Roadmap

Forward-looking direction. Pair with tasks.md (active work) and memory.md (history).

## Vision

Beats and Backlog is a writing-first publication about video games, anime, and music, told through one lens — comfort, nostalgia, and limited hours — with a *Samurai Champloo* woodblock-and-vinyl aesthetic. It pays respect to the culture that built it and holds the door for who's coming up. Each page is its own bold, authentic world; the writing and the voice are the moat.

## Current Focus

**Theme:** Give every surface a distinct, authentic identity, then fill with real content.
**Goals:**
1. Bespoke "worlds" for the signature pages/columns (done: Home, Tribute, Nightcap, Comfort Mode, Backlog).
2. Make the reading experience and editorial pages as strong as the column landings.
